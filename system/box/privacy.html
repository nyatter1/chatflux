Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be collected in this talk community, how it is used, and what choices you have. The goal is to keep the service safe, reliable, and respectful.

1. Information You Provide

When you register or use the talk, you may provide a username, email address, password, profile details, avatar, cover image, messages, private messages, wall posts, reports, and uploaded files.

2. Technical Information

The system may collect technical data such as IP address, device/browser details, country or region, login time, session identifiers, security logs, and moderation records. This helps protect accounts, prevent spam, and detect abuse.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate the talk, display profiles and messages, maintain sessions, send notifications, process reports, enforce rules, improve safety, prevent fraud, and respond to support requests.

4. Cookies and Sessions

Cookies or similar session storage may be used to keep you logged in, remember preferences, protect forms, and improve security. Disabling cookies may prevent some features from working correctly.

5. Visibility of Content

Public room messages, public profiles, wall posts, and shared media may be visible to other members. Private messages are intended for selected users, but administrators may access records when required for safety, abuse investigation, legal compliance, or technical support.

6. Sharing and Security

Information is not sold to advertisers. Data may be shared only with trusted service providers, when required by law, or when necessary to protect users and the service. Reasonable security measures are used, but no online service can guarantee perfect security.

7. Your Choices

You may update your profile, change privacy settings, delete certain content where the feature is available, or contact the administrator for help with account access, correction, or deletion requests.

8. Children and Updates

This community is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required by local law. This policy may be updated when features, legal requirements, or safety practices change.
